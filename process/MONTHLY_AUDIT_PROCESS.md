# 30-Day Recurring SEO Audit Process

## 🎯 1. Purpose & Business Context
SEO is not a one-time project; it requires continuous adjustments. Search engine algorithms change, competitors publish new content, product features evolve, and new technical bugs can occur during product releases.

This **Monthly Audit Process** establishes a structured 30-day operational routine. It divides the audit tasks into weekly focus blocks to ensure we continuously monitor site performance, keep content up to date, and resolve technical issues quickly.

---

## 🗓️ 2. The 30-Day Recurring Timeline

```text
  [WEEK 1] ─────────────► [WEEK 2] ─────────────► [WEEK 3] ─────────────► [WEEK 4]
  Technical Crawl         Content & Keyword       Triage & Jira           Reporting &
  & Health Check          Gap Analysis            Ticket Assignment       Baseline Logging
```

### Week 1: Crawl & Technical Auditing (Days 1 - 7)
*   **Focus:** Inspect site health, resolve crawl issues, and check indexation.
*   **Step-by-Step Instructions:**
    1.  **Day 1:** Run a site crawl using Screaming Frog as outlined in [TOOLS_GUIDE.md](file:///d:/4DK/Projects/Marketting/seo-content-audit-strategy/tools/TOOLS_GUIDE.md).
    2.  **Day 3:** Filter the crawl data for redirect loops, 404/500 errors, and canonical issues.
    3.  **Day 5:** Inspect Google Search Console for new indexing errors, sitemap warnings, or mobile usability issues.
    4.  **Day 7:** Record the initial technical metrics (LCP, page speeds, crawl error counts) in the [BASELINE_TEMPLATE.csv](file:///d:/4DK/Projects/Marketting/seo-content-audit-strategy/kpis/BASELINE_TEMPLATE.csv).

### Week 2: Keyword Gap & Content Quality Auditing (Days 8 - 14)
*   **Focus:** Identify keyword opportunities and review page content.
*   **Step-by-Step Instructions:**
    1.  **Day 8:** Run the competitor keyword gap analysis report to identify high-value search queries.
    2.  **Day 10:** Review high-priority pages (Pricing, Feature pages) for word count, readability, and outdated product information.
    3.  **Day 12:** Review backlink profiles to identify and resolve broken links (404 pages with backlinks).
    4.  **Day 14:** Update the page grading metrics in the master sheet using the scoring formulas.

### Week 3: Triage & Developer/Content Sprint Planning (Days 15 - 21)
*   **Focus:** Prioritize issues and assign tasks to the team.
*   **Step-by-Step Instructions:**
    1.  **Day 15:** Group issues into the priority quadrants (Quick Wins vs. Major Projects).
    2.  **Day 17:** Host a sprint planning meeting with Content Writers and Developers to review priority tasks.
    3.  **Day 19:** Create corresponding tickets (Jira, Asana, or Trello) for the team.
    4.  **Day 21:** Distribute writing briefs for content expansions or updates.

### Week 4: Implementation Monitoring & Executive Reporting (Days 22 - 30)
*   **Focus:** Resolve Quick Wins, monitor developer tasks, and share reports with leadership.
*   **Step-by-Step Instructions:**
    1.  **Day 22-26:** Work through and resolve the Quick Win tasks (meta tags, simple internal links).
    2.  **Day 28:** Confirm that developers have scheduled the Major Projects (Core Web Vitals, site-wide caching) in the next development sprint.
    3.  **Day 30:** Compile the monthly summary using the [AUDIT_REPORT_TEMPLATE.md](file:///d:/4DK/Projects/Marketting/seo-content-audit-strategy/templates/AUDIT_REPORT_TEMPLATE.md) and share it with leadership.

## 🚫 4. Common Mistakes to Avoid
*   **Skipping Months Because Traffic is High:** Assuming the site is fine because traffic is steady. Technical bugs or search changes can occur at any time; monthly checkups help prevent sudden ranking drops.
*   **Setting Unrealistic Deadlines for Developers:** Creating technical tickets in Week 3 and expecting them to be resolved by Week 4. Developers work on sprint cycles; assign tickets ahead of time and consult with development leads.
*   **Not Documenting Completed Work:** Forgetting to update the status column in your tracking sheet. This makes it difficult to track what has been resolved and measure the ROI of your SEO efforts.

## 📅 5. Expected Outcomes & Timelines
*   **Month 1 (First Cycle):** Complete tool configurations, run the initial crawl, baseline site metrics, and resolve the first batch of Quick Wins.
*   **Month 2:** Establish the recurring routine, create development tickets, and launch the first set of content updates.
*   **Month 3 onwards:** Review completed sprint items, monitor keyword rank improvements, and update baseline tracking sheets.
*   **Expected Result:** A systematic, repeatable process that reduces technical errors, improves content quality, and increases organic search traffic.
